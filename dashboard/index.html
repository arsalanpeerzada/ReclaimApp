How these numbers are made

      
    
        
  • Only people who opted in are counted. Each phone uploads one summary per finished day under an anonymous id: reel totals, budget, quests, pause outcomes and, with a separate yes, mood and WHO-5 scores. Nothing about what they watched.
    • 
        
  • Reel change: average of the first 7 shared days against the latest 7, for people with at least 14 days.
    • 
        
  • Pause stop rate: share of mindful pauses where the person chose "I'm done for now".
    • 
        
  • Retention: of people who started at least 7 (or 30) days ago, how many still shared a day at that point. Graduates stop on purpose.
    • 
        
  • Hours won back: reels below each person's own usual day, at about 5 seconds a reel.
    • 
        
  • Opt-in users are self-selected, so these show association, not cause. A controlled study would be needed for causal claims.
